~/about

A bit about me and what I work with.

I'm Mohammed Ismail Shaikh, a Python developer and RPA specialist with over 10 years of making operations faster through practical, scalable automation.

Robotic Process Automation is where I built my foundation. I take a process from first discussion to a running bot: finding what's worth automating, designing it, documenting it in PDD and SDD, and building it in Automation Anywhere, UiPath or Power Automate. For smaller jobs I reach for Excel macros, VBA, VB Script and Power Query.

Most of my work is backend engineering with Django, Flask and FastAPI: clean APIs, solid data models and production-ready apps on PostgreSQL, MySQL and MS SQL. I pair that with Python ETL pipelines and n8n workflows that clean up unstructured data and connect systems.

Right now I'm going deep on Generative AI and Agentic AI. I've built a RAG application with LangChain and I'm exploring how AI agents use tools, chain steps and work together. I started out in electronics, designing embedded and IoT systems, and I speak English, Hindi and Urdu.

~/rpa

How I take a process from manual to automated.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Sit with the people doing the work, map every step and pick the processes where a bot saves the most time.

  2. 02

    Design

    Write the PDD, agree on the rules and exceptions, and plan how the bot handles messy or missing data.

  3. 03

    Build

    Develop the bot in Automation Anywhere, UiPath or Power Automate, with Python or VBA where a tool falls short.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Test against real cases, document the build in an SDD and keep it running as the process changes.
